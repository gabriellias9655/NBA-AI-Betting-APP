World Cup 2026 Lab — Mac Install Guide
======================================

WHAT YOU NEED
-------------
- macOS 11 or later (Apple Silicon or Intel Mac)
- Internet connection (first launch downloads the prediction engine)


DOWNLOAD
--------
Download the file named something like:

  World Cup 2026 Lab-1.0.0.dmg

Use the official download link only.


INSTALL (RECOMMENDED)
---------------------
1. Open your Downloads folder.
2. Double-click the .dmg file (e.g. World Cup 2026 Lab-1.0.0.dmg).
3. A window opens showing:
   - World Cup 2026 Lab
   - Install.command
   - Applications (shortcut)
4. Double-click "Install.command".
5. If macOS asks whether to open it, click Open.
6. Wait until you see "Done. Opening World Cup 2026 Lab..."
7. The app is installed to Applications and should open automatically.


IF macOS SAYS THE APP IS "DAMAGED" OR WON'T OPEN
------------------------------------------------
This is normal for apps downloaded from the web without Apple signing.
The app is not broken.

Try this:

1. Open Terminal (Applications → Utilities → Terminal).
2. Paste this line and press Enter:

   xattr -cr "/Applications/World Cup 2026 Lab.app"

3. Open World Cup 2026 Lab from Applications.

Or:

1. Open Applications.
2. Right-click (or Control-click) World Cup 2026 Lab.
3. Click Open.
4. Click Open again in the dialog.
5. After the first time, you can open it normally.


MANUAL INSTALL (WITHOUT Install.command)
----------------------------------------
1. Open the .dmg.
2. Drag "World Cup 2026 Lab" into Applications.
3. Open Terminal and run:

   xattr -cr "/Applications/World Cup 2026 Lab.app"

4. Open the app from Applications.


FIRST LAUNCH
------------
1. The app may show a setup screen while it downloads Python and
   prediction libraries (one-time, a few minutes).
2. Keep your Mac connected to the internet.
3. When setup finishes, the World Cup dashboard opens.

If setup fails, quit the app, check your connection, and open it again.
Use "Try again" on the loading screen if it appears.


UNINSTALL
---------
1. Quit World Cup 2026 Lab.
2. Drag World Cup 2026 Lab from Applications to the Trash.
3. Optional — remove app data (downloads Python/runtime data):

   rm -rf ~/Library/Application\ Support/world-cup-2026-desktop


QUICK SUMMARY
-------------
Download the .dmg → open it → double-click Install.command →
use the app from Applications.

If blocked, run in Terminal:

   xattr -cr "/Applications/World Cup 2026 Lab.app"
